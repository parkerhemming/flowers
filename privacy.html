> privacy

plain words, no fine print. last updated november 2026.

what we keep

  • the bouquet: their name, your name if you add it, the flowers you picked and your notes. we keep it so the link keeps working.
  • your email: only to send you your link, once. no newsletters, no marketing.
  • a little picture of the bouquet, used for its home-screen icon and link previews.

what we don't

  • no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. the only cookies are stripe's (set during checkout to stop card fraud) and cloudflare's security check.
  • nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • to stop abuse, the server briefly counts how often each visitor starts a checkout. those counts are forgotten within an hour.

who helps

  • stripe handles payments. your card details go straight to them and never touch this site. see stripe's privacy policy.
  • microsoft 365 sends the confirmation email.
  • cloudflare sits in front of the site to keep it fast and safe.

who can see a bouquet

anyone you give the link to. links are random and not listed anywhere, but treat them like a card: whoever has it can open it.

refunds

not happy with your bouquet? email flowers@hemm.ing and you'll get your $0.99 back. no questions asked.

deleting a bouquet

want a bouquet or your email removed? email flowers@hemm.ing with the link and it'll be deleted for good.
